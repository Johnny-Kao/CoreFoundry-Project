# Funding

This research program is maintained independently.

Funding does not purchase roadmap control, priority issue handling, private support, or guaranteed work on a particular library.

It increases the amount of upstream engineering capacity I can sustain.

## What funding supports

- benchmarking and controlled A/B validation;
- CI and multi-platform testing;
- sanitizers and correctness validation;
- compute and test environments;
- AI-assisted engineering and research tooling;
- time spent reproducing difficult behavior;
- upstream issue / PR preparation;
- follow-up benchmarking and maintainer review work.

## Funding principles

1. **Upstream first**  
   Improvements should land in the projects where downstream users can benefit broadly.

2. **Evidence before claims**  
   Performance work should be benchmarked and correctness boundaries should be explicit.

3. **No paid priority access**  
   Sponsors may share infrastructure priorities and demand signals, but sponsorship does not guarantee work on a particular request.

4. **Public benefit**  
   The default goal is broadly useful upstream improvement rather than private feature development.
