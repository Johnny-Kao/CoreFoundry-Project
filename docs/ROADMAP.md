# Roadmap

This roadmap describes research direction rather than guaranteed deliverables.

## Near term

- maintain a public record of merged upstream impact;
- identify and validate additional zero-incremental-resource optimization opportunities;
- deepen work across Python scientific, networking, compression, profiling, and concurrency infrastructure;
- collect infrastructure-priority signals from real users and organizations;
- improve reproducibility of benchmark evidence.

## Medium term

- connect findings across projects instead of treating each optimization as isolated;
- identify recurring classes of unnecessary work across dependency layers;
- develop clearer heuristics for where already-paid costs can be reused safely;
- study how low-level efficiency changes compound through cloud and AI workloads;
- expand research into execution routing and heterogeneous compute.

## Long term

The broader goal is to help make widely used infrastructure materially more efficient and robust before additional compute is added.

The research program is intentionally upstream-oriented: small changes at foundational layers can have disproportionate downstream impact.
